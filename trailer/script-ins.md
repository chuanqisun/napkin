# Napkin — Instagram Reels (9:16) Trailer Script & Shot Choreography

> **Status:** Draft v1 — Ready for Review (Step 1a: Script & Asset Prep)  
> **Format:** **9:16 Vertical (1080×1920 @ 60fps)** — captured at **1440×2560** (720×1280 @ 2× HiDPI) so every 2.5× crash zoom stays razor-sharp  
> **Target Runtime:** **21.5 seconds** (strictly within 15–25s — zero filler, zero slow fades, built for immediate scroll-stop)  
> **Core Rule:** **Show, Don't Tell.** One razor-sharp **Rule-of-Three comedic arc** (*Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3: ruin it → Crumple → Eureka!*) using **28 words of VO total**, letting the tactile visuals, camera snaps, and in-game audio hit like a punchline every 3–4 seconds.

## 1. Vertical 9:16 Strategy & Instagram Safe Zones

- **Scroll-Stop First 1.0 Second:** No fade-in, no title card, no blank canvas pause. Frame 1 opens mid-action with the **Pen Menu snapping open**, a crisp UI click, loud fountain-nib ASMR, and immediate motion.
- **Vertical Flowchart Composition:** All diagrams flow **top-to-bottom** (not wide left-to-right) so words like `Big Idea`, `Magic`, and `SHIP IT!` stay huge and instantly readable on a phone screen.
- **Reels UI Safe Zone (`1080×1920`):**
  - **Keep clear — Top 14% (`Y < 270px`):** Reels header/camera icons.
  - **Keep clear — Bottom 22% (`Y > 1500px`):** Caption, handle & audio bar.
  - **Keep clear — Bottom-Right 15% (`X > 920px, Y > 1050px`):** Like / Comment / Share stack.
  - **Sweet Spot:** All diagrams, stains, crumple center, and the Steam CTA sit cleanly inside `Y = 300px–1440px`. The café mug rests naturally in the **upper-right** (`~22%` from top) — completely clear of Reels buttons!
- **Mute-Friendly Punch:** Works 100% on mute (huge handwritten gags + snappy kinetic subtitles centered cleanly above the bottom safe line), and hits twice as hard with audio on.

## 3. Second-by-Second Shot Choreography (21.5s)

### Beat 1 (`0:00.0 – 0:03.5`) — **SELECTING PEN** *(Instant Scroll-Stop Hook)*
- **Action:** Hard open on a warm embossed kraft napkin (`Warm Kraft`) with the **Pen & Writing Style** popover snapping open. Cursor flicks right past *Ballpoint* & *Pencil*, deliberately clicks the gold-nibbed **`Fountain Pen`** + **`Designer Cursive`**, then immediately drops onto the center napkin:
  - Freehand draws a wobbly oval → *SNAP!* auto-beautifies into crisp organic ink → live cursive streams **`Big Idea`**.
  - Quick downward arrow ↓ → box → **`Magic`** (blue ink).
- **Cursor:** Snappy, confident arc — zero wasted travel time.
- **Camera:** Starts tight (`1.55×`) on the Pen menu click → **fast vertical whip-pan down** tracking the cursor straight onto the napkin (`1.3×`), gliding slightly downward with the strokes.
- **Audio:** Punchy café-bossa guitar groove starts immediately on beat 1 (`~116 BPM`, crisp brushed snare) + loud fountain-nib paper-scratch ASMR. **VO-1** (`0:00.2`).

---

### Beat 2 (`0:03.5 – 0:07.0`) — **ORDER DRINK** *(Block → Caffeine Rescue)*
- **Action:**
  - Cursor starts a confused squiggle beneath `Magic` → **Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`) → game lets out weary **`"Hmmmm..."`** sigh (`sigh_block`).
  - Cursor perks up and darts straight to the pulsing amber **`Menu`** button → **Café Menu** pops open displaying all 4 lush top-down drinks → taps **`Coffee Latte`**.
  - Popover closes; the ceramic **Coffee Latte** glides smoothly onto the upper-right corner of the vertical napkin and lands with a weighty **clink** (`cup_setdown`).
- **Cursor:** Quick defeated pause → sudden energetic beeline to `Menu`.
- **Camera:** Tight **whip-zoom (`1.65×`)** up-right onto the pulsing `Menu` button & Café Menu grid → **snap pull-out (`1.05×`)** framing the mug gliding in and landing.
- **Audio:** Beat briefly dips for the sigh, then snaps back on the ceramic *clink*! **VO-2** (`0:04.2`).

---

### Beat 3 (`0:07.0 – 0:11.0`) — **SPILL / DRIP** *(Hero Comedy Punchline)*
- **Action:**
  - Cursor clicks the **Coffee Latte** → mug lifts off-screen → loud, blissful **`slurp… "Aaaahhh..."`** (`sip_ahhh`).
  - **`0:08.3` — THE SPLAT:** Mid-sip, a dark wet **coffee splatter trail** explodes right across **`Magic`** in the dead center of the napkin, visibly bleeding and drying into the fibers!
  - Deadpan game vocal: **`"Oops!"`** (`oops_drip`).
  - Mug drops innocently back down (`65%` full, *clink*). Cursor drags/swivels the mug a couple inches sideways — **revealing a brand-new dark circular coffee ring** right underneath!
- **Cursor:** Happy click on mug → freezes dead-still on splat → slow, miserable drag of the mug.
- **Camera:**
  - `0:08.3`: **HARD CRASH ZOOM (`1.1× → 2.55×` in 5 frames!)** slamming dead-center onto the glistening coffee drip over `Magic`, holding frozen for `0.8s`!
  - `0:09.5`: Snappy whip-cut (`1.85×`) onto the freshly revealed **coffee ring** as the mug drags off it!
- **Audio:** Music **HARD-CUTS TO DEAD SILENCE** on the exact splat frame! Only wet drip → `"Oops!"` → ceramic *clink* → dry **VO-3** (`0:09.6`).

---

### Beat 4 (`0:11.0 – 0:14.8`) — **TRASH AN IDEA** *(Visceral 3D Crumple Reset)*
- **Action:**
  - Cursor darts straight up to the **Trash / Crumple** icon and clicks!
  - **The Charming Gag:** First, the latte mug *politely lifts itself off the napkin* out of harm's way (0.35s).
  - **`0:11.8` — THE CRUMPLE:** The entire stained napkin violently crunches inward in **3D triangulated mesh physics**, shudders, and **launches spinning off the right edge of the vertical screen** — exposing a spotless new napkin (`Diner Ivory`, stitched border) underneath!
  - Latte mug glides serenely back down onto the fresh paper (*clink!*).
- **Cursor:** Frantic sprint to Trash → parks cleanly while the 3D ball flies.
- **Camera:** Quick cursor-follow snap up to Trash → **immediate pull wide (`1.0×`)** for maximum 3D crumple spectacle + **3–5px impact micro-shake** synced to the crunch beats → subtle pan-right as the ball yeets off-frame.
- **Audio:** Loud real paper crunch (`crumble.mp3`) + anguished **`"AAAARGH!"`** (`argh_stain`) → whoosh → ceramic *clink* → music drops back in with extra energy! **VO-4** (`0:13.4`).

### Technical Capture Details for 9:16
1. **Viewport & Sharp Zooms:** Capture at logical **720×1280** with `deviceScaleFactor: 2` (**1440×2560** raw video @ 60fps). Why `720px` width? It stays cleanly above the `680px` mobile wrap threshold (single sleek toolbar, generous napkin canvas, zero awkward overflow) while giving **huge 2.5× zoom headroom** down to the final **1080×1920** Instagram Reels master!
2. **Cursor & Virtual Camera Telemetry:** Record exact `(t, cursorX, cursorY)` per frame during Playwright capture so Step 2 applies smooth spring-damped **vertical cursor-following**, **whip-pans**, **5-frame crash zooms**, and **crumple micro-shake** with frame-accurate timing.
3. **Deterministic Comedy Hits:** Inject a capture-only RNG/hook shim via Playwright (zero permanent source changes) so the Latte sip *always* splatters right on `Magic`, dragging *always* leaves a dark ring, and the exact paper textures (`Warm Kraft` → `Diner Ivory`) appear on cue.
4. **Steam-Clean Toolbar:** Hide `#github-link` via capture-only CSS injection so the UI stays sleek and focused.
