# Napkin — Steam Promotional Trailer Script & Shot Choreography

> **Status:** Draft v1 — Ready for Review (Step 1a: Script & Asset Prep Plan)  
> **Target Runtime:** ~60 seconds (58–62s) · **Format:** 1920×1080 @ 60fps (captured at 2× HiDPI / 1440p+ for razor-sharp crash zooms)  
> **Core Principle:** **Show, Don't Tell.** Zero feature-list narration, zero cold logo bumpers. Tell one funny, relatable 60-second mini-story — *"The Anatomy of a 'Billion-Dollar' Café Idea"* — while the visuals and in-game audio organically showcase every tactile detail.

## 1. Creative Direction & Comedy Tone

### The Premise
A sleek, deadpan "prestige productivity commercial" that gets hilariously derailed by real café-table chaos — messy drafts, desperate caffeine breaks, catastrophic coffee drips, rage-crumpling the napkin, and an accidental flash of brilliance.

### Why the Audio Contrast Works
The game already has wonderfully theatrical companion vocal clips (`sigh_block`, `sip_ahhh`, `oops_drip`, `argh_stain`, `ooo_intrigued`, `aha_spark`, `eureka_win`) plus velocity-matched pen-on-paper ASMR foley and ceramic mug clinks.
- **Voice-Over Persona:** Calm, warm, slightly posh, dryly amused narrator (think a minimalist design commercial or dry indie documentary). **Only ~58 words total** across the whole minute.
- **Golden Rule of Pacing:** The narrator *never* talks over the in-game vocal punchlines (`"Oops!"`, `"AAAARGH!"`, `"EUREKA!"`) or ASMR moments. Silence + hard music cuts let the game's own personality steal the laugh.
- **Music Bed:** Cozy, brushed-snare café acoustic guitar / light rhodes jazz (~104 BPM) with a warm room tone — **hard-cutting to dead silence** on the coffee-drip crash zoom and the rage-crumple windup, then kicking back in with snappy rhythm for the montage and climax.

## 3. Beat-by-Beat Storyboard, Choreography & Camera Direction

### ACT I — THE HOOK & THE "GENIUS" PLAN (`0:00 – 0:11`)

#### Shot 1 (`0:00 – 0:04`) — Signature Moment #1: **Selecting Pen**
- **On-Screen Action:** Opens immediately on a fresh, warm embossed kraft napkin (`Warm Kraft`, subtle crease & border, no splash screen). Cursor glides organically up to the toolbar **Pen** button and clicks to open the **Pen & Writing Style** popover. Cursor hovers briefly over `Ballpoint Pen` (0.3s beat), glides past `2B Graphite Pencil`, pauses decisively on the gold-nibbed **`Fountain Pen`**, clicks it, then selects **`Designer Cursive`** (live handwritten `"Napkin"` preview cards visible) and closes popover.
- **Cursor Choreography:** Deliberate, silky Bezier arc with a subtle comedic micro-hesitation on `Ballpoint` → decisive flick down to `Fountain Pen` (shows personality through mouse movement alone!).
- **Camera Movement:** Starts at `1.25×` warm macro framing on the blank napkin texture → **cursor-follow pan up** to the toolbar as popover opens → crisp **1.45× punch-in** centered on the `Fountain Pen` row on click → smooth pull-back (`1.15×`) sweeping down to center-left of the napkin.
- **Audio:** Soft café ambient hum + warm acoustic guitar intro enters; crisp click + fountain-nib tap. **VO-01** (`0:00.5`).

#### Shot 2 (`0:04 – 0:11`) — Freehand Auto-Beautify & Live Neural Cursive
- **On-Screen Action:** Using **Sketch (`S`)** + **Fountain Pen (Blue ink)**:
  1. Cursor freehand-draws a slightly wobbly oval on the left — pauses 0.6s — *SNAP!* Auto-beautifies into designer-grade organic ink with subtle under-sketch.
  2. Single-click inside oval → types **`Big Idea`** (neural cursive streams out live stroke-by-stroke!).
  3. Freehand arrow right → rough rectangle → *SNAP!* crisp box → types **`Magic`**.
  4. Arrow right → clicks **Red ink swatch** → rough oval → *SNAP!* → types **`Profit`**.
  5. Pause 0.3s → clicks beneath `Magic` → types **`figure out later`** in red cursive.
- **Cursor Choreography:** Expressive, confident human hand-speed strokes (fast sweep on curves, slight deceleration at corners); quick decisive flick to the Red swatch.
- **Camera Movement:** Smooth **cursor-follow tracking camera** (`1.3×`) gliding left-to-right with the pen tip as `Big Idea → Magic → Profit` comes alive → snaps into a cheeky **1.55× zoom-in** specifically on **`figure out later`** as the last word finishes streaming.
- **Audio:** Velocity-matched metallic fountain-nib paper-scratch ASMR + rhythmic pen typing pulses; game's `"Mm-hmm..."` (`mm_ponder`) gently murmurs on `Profit`. **VO-02** (`0:04.8`).

### Technical Capture Notes (How We'll Make It Look Like a Studio Trailer)
1. **Ultra-Sharp Zooms (Zero Pixelation):** Capture the live Vite app at **2560×1440** (1280×720 logical CSS viewport with `deviceScaleFactor: 2` — keeps toolbar & handwriting crisp and super readable even in Steam's small store embed, while giving **2.0×–2.6× headroom** for crash zooms when rendering the final 1920×1080 60fps master!).
2. **Smooth Human Cursor & Virtual Camera Telemetry:** Our capture script drives real pointer events along eased Bezier trajectories with natural human hand speed, recording `(t, cursorX, cursorY)` telemetry per frame so Step 2's compositor can apply silky **spring-damped cursor-follow panning**, **crash zooms**, and **crumple micro-shake** with frame-accurate precision.
3. **Deterministic Comedy Timing:** Without permanently changing app source code, the Playwright capture harness injects a small test-only RNG/hook shim so the latte *always* triggers the exact wet splatter trail on cue over `"Magic"`, the drag *always* leaves the coffee ring, and the exact napkin palettes (`Warm Kraft`, `Diner Ivory`, `Toasted Oat`, `Cafe Ecru`) appear cleanly per scene.
4. **Audio Separation:** Capture clean game SFX & synthesized pen foley stems alongside exact event timestamps so Step 2 can mix VO, music hard-cuts, pen ASMR, and companion voices (`"Oops!"`, `"AAAARGH!"`, `"EUREKA!!"`) with broadcast-level clarity (LUFS-normalized for Steam).
5. **Steam Toolbar Polish (Optional):** We can optionally hide the `#github-link` icon in the top-right toolbar during capture via CSS injection so the Steam trailer looks 100% native to Steam.
